open desensitizer

A dot moves from left to right. Your eyes follow it. That is the whole tool.

How it works

Bilateral stimulation alternates a stimulus between the left and the right side: here a point that the eyes track across the screen, and optionally a tone that moves between the ears in step with it. The eye movement resembles the rapid eye movement of dreaming sleep, and the same left-right alternation is the mechanical part of EMDR therapy.

Used on its own, it tends to settle the nervous system. Used while holding a difficult memory or feeling in mind, it can make that memory easier to carry. Sessions are short; ten to fifteen minutes is plenty.

Everything runs in this page. Nothing is sent anywhere, and the settings stay in your browser.

Before you start

  • This is a self-help tool, not therapy. It does not replace treatment for trauma.
  • Do not use it with photosensitive epilepsy. With a dissociative disorder, ask a clinician first.
  • Sit comfortably, keep your head still, and move only your eyes. Use headphones if you turn the tone on.
  • If a session becomes too intense, press Space. The movement stops and a breathing exercise takes over.

Session

During a session: Space for grounding, Esc to stop, F for fullscreen. One full cycle per second is 1 Hz; most people settle between 0.5 and 1 Hz.
